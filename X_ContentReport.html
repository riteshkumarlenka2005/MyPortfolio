Chapter 08 — Full About Page

The story behind the work.

The full About page is only opened intentionally — by people who are already invested. This is where you can go deeper. Three sections: Who, How, Why.

Full About Page · Complete Content
Three-Part Story Structure
Timeless · No Dates or Numbers
Page Headline
Not just an engineer. A person who builds with intention.
Section 01 · Who

I'm Ritesh Kumar Lenka — a Computer Science engineer from Odisha, India, working at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Full Stack development. I study at Gandhi Institute of Engineering and Technology, where I'm pursuing my degree while simultaneously building and shipping real products that live on the internet and serve real users.

Section 02 · How I Think

My approach to any problem begins with a question I can't leave unanswered. I don't move toward a solution until I understand the problem at its root — not its surface. This makes me slow in some rooms, and invaluable in others. I research deeply, build deliberately, and ship only when the work is genuinely ready. Every decision is calculated. Every line of code has a reason. I am not interested in building things quickly — I am interested in building things that last.

Section 03 · Why It Matters

I came into engineering because I wanted to understand how the world is shaped by the systems inside it. AI is not just a technology to me — it is a lens through which genuinely hard human problems become solvable. The projects I've built reflect this: simulating human emotion to educate people about cyber threats, building systems that read interview performance across multiple human signals at once, creating platforms that connect people to opportunities they couldn't easily access before. The common thread is not the technology — it is the intent to make something real, and make it matter.

Closing Line (Page Footer of About)

I'm always open to conversations about ambitious problems, interesting ideas, and the kind of work that's worth doing well.

Structure note: Three sections, no headers needed — the flow speaks for itself. Total word count: ~280 words. This is the maximum for a full About page. It respects the reader's time while giving depth to those who want it. No CGPA, no semester, no year — fully timeless.
